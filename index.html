📺 Show the slideshow on a TV (no projector, no cable)

From a Windows laptop to a smart TV (Miracast)

  1. Turn on the TV and choose its Screen Mirroring / Screen Share input.
  2. On the laptop press Windows + K and pick the TV name.
  3. Come back here and click Start full screen below.

With Chromecast / Android TV / Google TV

  1. Laptop and TV must be on the same Wi‑Fi.
  2. In Chrome click the ⋮ menu → Cast… → Sources → Cast tab, then pick the TV.
  3. Click Start full screen below. Music plays through the TV.

From an Android phone

  1. Open this slideshow on the phone.
  2. Swipe down → Smart View / Screen Cast / Cast → choose the TV.
  3. Turn the phone sideways and click Start full screen.

Wall / Projector setup

1) Connect the projector (HDMI). 2) Press Windows + P → Duplicate. 3) Click 📽 Projector. Point the projector at the front wall; use Keystone if the picture is wider at the top or bottom. Settings are remembered on this laptop.
